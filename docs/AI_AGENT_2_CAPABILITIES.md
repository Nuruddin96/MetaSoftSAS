# AI Agent 2.0 — Capabilities & Limitations

Written for a store owner, not a developer. If you want the technical detail behind any claim here, see `AI_AGENT_2_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## What the AI CAN do

- **Maintain conversation context.** If a customer says "আমি COSRX Snail Cream নিতে চাই" and then, in a later message, just asks "দাম কত?", the AI understands they mean the COSRX Snail Cream — it does not ask "কোন প্রোডাক্টের কথা বলছেন?" when the answer is already in the conversation.
- **Understand follow-up questions** in general, not just that one example — it reads the whole conversation before answering, not just the latest message.
- **Use your written instructions.** Whatever you type into the AI Instructions field in your panel settings is followed as a real business rule for every reply.
- **Learn how your own staff actually talk.** It studies real replies your human staff have sent (never its own past replies) and matches your typical length, tone, emoji habit, and use of "আপু"/"ভাইয়া" — instead of sounding like a generic script.
- **Use verified customer memory.** If a returning customer messages you again, it knows their most recent order number, status, and delivery address — without asking them to repeat it — but only when their identity is verified by the channel itself (their real Messenger/WhatsApp account), never by anything they typed in the chat.
- **Use your product and business knowledge when it's configured.** Real prices and stock levels from your own catalog, and your delivery charges, when a customer asks about something in your store.
- **Process images you're able to send it** — an image attached to the customer's current message is genuinely visible to it, the same way a staff member glancing at a photo would be.
- **Transcribe voice messages** into text and reply to them naturally, the same as if the customer had typed the message.
- **Notice when a customer has been waiting.** If someone has sent several messages in a row with no reply, it knows that (as a fact — how long, how many messages), and can acknowledge it naturally.
- **Use tools where authorized** — but only in the staff panel chat, talking to you or your staff, never in a live conversation with a customer. In the panel, it can look up real orders/products/customers/sales data, and can *propose* creating an order/product or sending an order to a courier — but never executes any of those until a staff member explicitly confirms.
- **Understand order history where identity is verified** — see "customer memory" above.
- **Respond naturally**, without robotic scripted phrases, matching whatever mix of Bengali/Banglish/English the customer used.
- **Hand off honestly when a human is genuinely needed.** If a customer asks to talk to a person, it says so honestly and stops auto-replying — it does not pretend a transfer happened when it didn't, and it does not keep answering once a real handoff exists.

## What it CANNOT guarantee

- **It does not automatically know every product fact.** If your product isn't in your catalog, or the customer refers to it in a way that doesn't match a product name in your store, it will not invent a price, size, or stock number — it will say it isn't sure rather than guess.
- **It cannot guarantee perfect image recognition.** If it can't process an attached photo (a technical failure — network issue, unsupported format, oversized file), it does not pretend to have seen it. If a caption came with the photo, it will still answer that; if there's no caption and the image genuinely couldn't be processed, it may not reply to that particular message at all rather than fabricate a response about a photo it never actually saw.
- **It cannot guarantee perfect voice transcription.** Speech-to-text can misrecognize words, especially numbers, product names, or people's names. If the transcript comes out garbled, it will say so honestly and ask the customer to repeat or type it rather than guess what was meant. If transcription fails entirely, the same "may not reply" behavior as a failed image applies.
- **Prompt-based humanization is not mathematically deterministic.** Instructions like "don't say আপু/ভাইয়া every time," "don't sound robotic," "match this business's tone" are *instructions given to the AI model*, not hard-coded rules the software enforces line by line. The model follows them very consistently in practice, but it is fundamentally a language model, not a fixed script — occasional deviation is possible, the same way a very well-trained new staff member might still occasionally phrase something differently than expected.
- **Model behavior can change.** OpenAI can update the underlying model at any time; behavior that was well-tuned today could shift slightly after a model update, which is why the "known facts only" and "never invent a number" rules are treated as the load-bearing safety guarantee — not tone/style, which is inherently softer.
- **Image-follow-up product matching has a known limitation.** If a customer sends a photo and, in a *later* message, asks "এটার দাম কত?", the AI answers using whatever it said about that photo in its own earlier reply — it does not re-look at the image itself (only the image attached to the *current* message is ever actually visible to it). If its earlier description of the photo didn't use your product's exact catalog name, the system's automatic "always pull the real, current price" safety net may not re-trigger on that follow-up the same way it reliably does when a customer types the product name directly. The AI is still instructed never to invent a price either way, but this specific path has one fewer layer of automatic double-checking than a typed product name does.
- **It does not falsely claim a human transfer occurred.** It will never say "I've notified our team" or "a staff member will contact you" unless that has genuinely, verifiably happened in the system at that moment.
- **It does not invent unavailable price/stock/order information.** If the data isn't in your store or isn't verifiable, it says so rather than guessing — this is one of the most heavily enforced rules in its instructions.
- **It cannot magically know facts that aren't in your store's data or your own written instructions.** Opening hours, payment methods, policies, your phone number, your address — none of this is answered unless you've configured it (see `AI_AGENT_2_TENANT_CONFIGURATION.md` for exactly how).
